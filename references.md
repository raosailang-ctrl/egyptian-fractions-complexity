# References

## Two-term Egyptian fractions

1. Tieling Chen and Reginald Koo, “Two-term Egyptian fractions,” *Notes on Number Theory and Discrete Mathematics* 19(2), 2013, pp. 15–25. Theorem 4 gives the coprime-divisor-pair criterion; Theorems 9–10 give special fixed-numerator criteria.
   - https://nntdm.net/papers/nntdm-19/NNTDM-19-2-15-25.pdf
   - https://nntdm.net/volume-19-2013/number-2/15-25/
2. David Eppstein, “Ten Algorithms for Egyptian Fractions,” *Mathematica in Education and Research* 4(2), 1995, pp. 5–15. Updated online notes:
   - https://ics.uci.edu/~eppstein/numth/egypt/
   - https://ics.uci.edu/~eppstein/numth/egypt/smallnum.html

## Square-modulus prime distribution

3. Stephan Baier and Liangyi Zhao, “Bombieri–Vinogradov type theorems for sparse sets of moduli,” *Acta Arithmetica* 125(2), 2006, pp. 187–201. Theorem 4 is the square-modulus average estimate used in the candidate randomized reduction; the paper notes ineffectivity due to Siegel–Walfisz.
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0602116
   - https://doi.org/10.4064/aa125-2-5
